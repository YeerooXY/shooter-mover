# Difficulty modes and challenge modifiers

## Status

Product-planning document only. Names, unlocks and multipliers remain open.

## Confirmed direction

- Difficulty should change how encounters feel, not only inflate enemy health.
- Higher difficulty should improve rewards while preserving readable telegraphs and fair counterplay.
- Weapon definitions and exact item identities should not be rewritten when difficulty changes.
- Difficulty must work with authored levels, enemy definitions and generic encounter systems.

## Working difficulty ladder

| Mode | Intended audience | Working identity |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | first playthrough and learning | full telegraphs, baseline enemy compositions and forgiving recovery |
| Veteran | experienced characters | denser encounters, faster decisions and more elite combinations |
| Nightmare | optimised builds | dangerous role combinations, shorter safe windows and stronger mode modifiers |
| Cataclysm | endgame challenge | highly coordinated enemy pressure, limited recovery and premium milestone rewards |

The final names may change. A fifth optional challenge tier can be added later if Cataclysm proves too broad.

## Meaningful scaling dimensions

Difficulty may adjust:

- enemy composition and role combinations;
- elite frequency;
- attack cadence within readable limits;
- projectile speed within reaction limits;
- telegraph duration without removing essential information;
- enemy movement confidence and repositioning;
- crowd-control resistance with caps;
- environmental hazards;
- player recovery opportunities;
- reward quality, box tier weighting and milestone rewards.

Health and damage can scale, but they should not carry the entire difficulty increase.

## Standard

### Purpose

Teach the game, weapon identities and enemy telegraphs.

### Working rules

- baseline enemy definitions;
- fewer overlapping specialist roles;
- generous checkpoints or recovery;
- ordinary drop tables;
- no severe economy penalties for failure.

## Veteran

### Purpose

Become the normal long-term mode for players who understand the systems.

### Working rules

- more mixed-role encounters;
- modestly stronger enemy health/damage;
- quicker attack recovery;
- improved elite and strongbox weighting;
- first appearance of optional room modifiers.

## Nightmare

### Purpose

Challenge coherent class, weapon and augment builds.

### Working rules

- specialists appear together intentionally;
- elites gain authored modifiers or stronger attack combinations;
- fewer safe recovery windows;
- downed/revive resources are more limited in multiplayer;
- improved high-tier reward chances;
- first-clear rewards are deterministic and meaningful.

## Cataclysm

### Purpose

Provide an endgame mode for mastery, co-op coordination and cap-level retention.

### Working rules

- advanced encounter compositions;
- high elite density without filling every room with bullet sponges;
- room modifiers are visible before commitment;
- boss mechanics gain additional patterns rather than only larger numbers;
- failure remains possible and meaningful;
- milestone rewards may include overclock cores, capstone blueprints or special boxes.

## Optional challenge modifiers

Modifiers can add replayability without creating a new difficulty tier for every variation.

### Enemy modifiers

- Armoured Front — some enemies gain directional armour.
- Accelerated — movement and attack recovery improve, while telegraphs remain intact.
- Reinforcement Protocol — bounded additional waves arrive under visible conditions.
- Linked Defence — enemies share a visible protective link until separated.
- Volatile Death — selected enemies create a clearly telegraphed death hazard.

### Player-pressure modifiers

- Limited Repair — healing resources recover more slowly.
- Unstable Ammunition — weapon heat/recovery management is stricter.
- Hazardous Ground — authored environmental danger zones activate.
- Attrition — repeated damage temporarily reduces maximum recovery, resetting after the mission.

### Reward modifiers

Each modifier may add a visible reward multiplier or improve a specific drop category. Hidden modifiers that only make enemies stronger should be avoided.

## Unlock proposal

A working progression:

- Standard available immediately.
- Veteran unlocks after completing an early campaign milestone or reaching a modest level.
- Nightmare unlocks after completing the campaign/major arc on Veteran or reaching an endgame threshold.
- Cataclysm unlocks after a Nightmare clear plus a cap-level or build-readiness milestone.

Players should not need to repeat every level on every difficulty merely to access core content.

## Failure and rewards

### Working proposal

- Collected run rewards become permanent only according to the mode's mission-result rules.
- Standard may preserve more partial progress.
- Higher difficulties can increase risk, but failure should not delete previously owned equipment.
- First-clear and milestone rewards are granted exactly once.
- Repeated clears use ordinary deterministic reward generation.

## Solo and multiplayer scaling

Difficulty and party-size scaling are separate concepts.

Party scaling may adjust:

- enemy count;
- health on large enemies/bosses;
- simultaneous role pressure;
- revive economy;
- objective timing.

It should not simply multiply every enemy statistic by player count. Solo players must not receive encounter patterns that require multiple simultaneous positions unless adapted generically.

## Open decisions

- Final difficulty names.
- Exact unlock requirements.
- Whether difficulty is selected per level, per campaign run or per party lobby.
- Which rewards are exclusive versus merely more likely.
- Whether modifiers are player-selected, randomly offered or tied to levels.
- Whether Cataclysm uses fixed handcrafted rules or rotating seasonal rule sets.
- Whether competitive leaderboards require fixed builds/seeds for fairness.
- How checkpoints and partial rewards differ by difficulty.
